🎓 Alumni Association Platform

A centralized digital platform designed to connect alumni, students, and administrators of Government Engineering College. The platform aims to strengthen alumni engagement, facilitate professional networking, support career development, and encourage contributions towards institutional growth.

📌 Project Overview

Alumni communities play a vital role in mentoring students, sharing professional opportunities, supporting institutional initiatives, and preserving connections with their alma mater.

The Alumni Association Platform brings these activities together in one responsive web application, providing a common space for alumni to connect, collaborate, and contribute.

🎯 Objectives

* Build a centralized and searchable alumni directory.
* Facilitate professional networking and mentorship.
* Enable job postings and applications within the alumni community.
* Simplify alumni event management and registrations.
* Support donation campaigns and contribution tracking.
* Showcase alumni achievements and success stories.
* Collect feedback and conduct surveys.
* Provide administrative tools for managing the platform.

## SCENARIO 1: CURRENT STUDENT JOURNEY

### Objective
Demonstrate onboarding, alumni networking, career opportunity discovery, event participation, and institutional feedback submission.

### Walkthrough Steps
1. **Sign In**: Navigate to `/login` and sign in using `rahul.verma@student.gec.edu`.
2. **Directory Lookup**:
   - Navigate to `/directory`.
   - Search for "Priya" or filter by department "Mechanical Engineering".
   - View Priya Sundaram's verified alumni profile and LinkedIn link.
3. **Career Exploration**:
   - Navigate to `/jobs`.
   - Filter by "Full-time" or search for "Robotics".
   - Open job details and submit an application with resume link and brief cover letter.
4. **Mentorship Pairing**:
   - Navigate to `/mentorship`.
   - Submit a mentorship pairing request to an alumni mentor with note explaining career objectives.
5. **Event Registration**:
   - Navigate to `/events`.
   - Select the "Robotics & Hardware Alumni Showcase 2026" and click "Register".
   - Verify registration status appears in `/my-events`.
6. **Submit Institutional Feedback**:
   - Click "Feedback" in the top navigation or navigate to `/feedback`.
   - Submit feedback on mentorship availability with a 5-star rating.

## SCENARIO 3: INSTITUTIONAL ADMINISTRATOR JOURNEY

### Objective
Demonstrate system-wide moderation, alumni verification, event scheduling, financial audit oversight, and content publishing.

### Walkthrough Steps
1. **Sign In**: Navigate to `/login` and sign in using `admin@gec.edu`.
2. **Access Admin Portal**:
   - Click "Admin Panel" in the header to enter `/admin`.
   - Review real-time dashboard KPIs across verification, events, jobs, and donations.
3. **Alumni Verification**:
   - Open `/admin/verification`.
   - Inspect pending registration requests and verify graduation credentials.
4. **Job Moderation**:
   - Open `/admin/jobs`.
   - Inspect job listings table, search by company, and deactivate outdated or non-compliant postings.
5. **Events Administration**:
   - Open `/admin/events`.
   - Click "+ Create Event" to schedule an upcoming campus reunion.
   - Inspect attendee rosters for existing events.
6. **Financial & Donation Oversight**:
   - Open `/admin/donations`.
   - Audit the verified transaction records log and total funds raised grouped by currency.
   - Inspect payment gateway order and payment ID metadata.
7. **Success Story Moderation**:
   - Open `/admin/success-stories`.
   - Review Priya Sundaram's submitted story in the queue.
   - Click "Approve & Publish" to push it live to `/stories`.
8. **Feedback & Survey Console**:
   - Open `/admin/surveys`.
   - Review student feedback tickets and transition ticket status from `PENDING` to `RESOLVED`.
